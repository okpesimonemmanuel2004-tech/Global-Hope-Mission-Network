Who we are

Global Hope Evangelistic Mission Network is a Christ-centered, inter-denominational missionary organisation committed to proclaiming the gospel of Jesus Christ, reaching the lost, making disciples and mobilizing believers for the fulfilment of the Great Commission. We exist to inspire hope, transform lives through the power of the gospel, and equip men and women to become effective witnesses for Christ.

Through evangelism, missions, discipleship, leadership development, youth outreach and strategic partnership with churches and like-minded ministries, we are raising and deploying passionate soul winners to communities and nations where Christ is yet to be fully known.

Our vision and mission

Our vision and mission

Everything we do is anchored in a clear, God-given vision and a bold, Christ-centered mission.

Our Vision

To see every community reached with the Gospel and every believer equipped to become an effective witness for Christ until the nations have heard.

Our Mission

To mobilize, train and deploy Soul Winners while advancing the gospel through Evangelism, Discipleship, Missions and Leadership Development.

how we work
